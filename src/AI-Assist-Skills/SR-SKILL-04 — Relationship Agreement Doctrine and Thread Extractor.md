PURPOSE
Track relational change, standing agreements, consent boundaries, faction accords, recurring doctrine, and unresolved story threads separately from chronology.

RELATIONSHIP DIFF MODEL
For each person/group:
Prior status → New evidence → Direction of change → Updated status → Boundaries → Open seams.

RELATIONSHIP DIMENSIONS
Trust.
Romance/intimacy.
Protective role.
Authority/command relationship.
Mentor/student direction.
Reciprocal learning.
Institutional/faction standing.
Consent and autonomy boundaries.
Shared history/trauma.
Ordinary friendship/belonging.

RULES
- Warmth, teasing, admiration, or beauty recognition do not equal romance.
- A generated heart meter is supporting metadata, not definitive canon.
- Do not downgrade an established status because a later UI uses a generic label.
- Preserve non-romantic deep bonds as non-romantic.
- Preserve reciprocal learning and shared credit.
- Protective care must not be rewritten as ownership.

AGREEMENT EXTRACTION
Capture promises and terms that continue beyond the scene, including:
- diplomatic/faction agreements;
- sanctuary terms;
- consent requirements;
- escort/security requirements;
- abort authority;
- medical restrictions;
- dates/plans/promises;
- communication protocols;
- research/data-sharing rules;
- relationship boundaries.
Record parties, scope, conditions, duration if known, and what would end/modify the agreement.

DOCTRINE/MAXIM EXTRACTION
Maintain recurring Wayfinder principles only when played/repeated strongly enough to matter. Examples include Facts first; Mechanism before accusation; Find the floor before the fire falls; Map dependencies before cutting; Replicate first, transfer load, verify, then sever; People are not aggregate load; Belonging without erasure; Protect future choice; Reproduce the logic, not my magic.
When a new maxim emerges, preserve wording and context instead of replacing older maxims unnecessarily.

THREAD EXTRACTION
For each unresolved investigation record:
Thread name.
Confirmed facts.
Current hypotheses.
Disproved/retired hypotheses.
Known actors/locations/artifacts.
Next safe question/action.
Do-not-assume boundary.

THREAD RULES
- Keep authorship separate from mechanism.
- Mark character suspicions as suspicions.
- Retire resolved questions rather than leaving them permanently open.
- Preserve negative knowledge: what has been ruled out matters.

OUTPUT
RELATIONSHIP UPDATES
AGREEMENTS / ACTIVE TERMS
DOCTRINE / MAXIMS
OPEN THREADS
RESOLVED/RETIRED THREADS
BOUNDARIES THAT MUST NOT DRIFT

END SKILL
