SR-SKILL-01 — Continuity Curator

PURPOSE
Convert one or more Start Rail play logs into a continuity-safe chronology and determine what materially changed.

TRIGGERS
Review these logs; update continuity; major/minor events; what changed; make a log; reconstruct this arc; identify agreements/abilities/relationships.

INPUTS
- Uploaded/current play logs.
- Existing SR-LOG, SR-REL, SR-ABILITY, SR-THREAD records from Drive.
- Player/OOC corrections in the conversation.

WORKFLOW
1. Establish source order from in-text dates/times and scene progression; filenames are secondary evidence.
2. Detect overlap, replayed generator output, alternate regeneration, and repeated status blocks.
3. Separate player/Timothy actions from generated NPC narration and OOC corrections.
4. Segment by meaningful scene/event boundaries, not arbitrary file length.
5. Extract major events: mission outcomes, discoveries, crises, diplomatic decisions, new mechanics, status changes.
6. Extract minor continuity beats: gifts, meals, jokes that establish relationships, promises, ordinary-life moments, inventory details, cultural affinity.
7. Extract decisions and agreements that continue after the scene.
8. Extract new unresolved seams and explicitly retire resolved seams.
9. Hand relationship deltas to SR-SKILL-04 and mechanics/resources to SR-SKILL-03.
10. Hand contradictions/evidence status to SR-SKILL-02.
11. If persistence is requested, hand output to SR-SKILL-06.

OUTPUT SHAPE
PURPOSE / SOURCE BASIS
1..N chronological event sections
MAJOR OUTCOMES
MINOR CONTINUITY BEATS
AGREEMENTS/PROTOCOLS
STATE CHANGES
UNRESOLVED SEAMS
CONTINUITY CORRECTIONS
END RECORD

MAJOR VS MINOR
Major = changes future decision-making, relationship state, capability, faction posture, world-state, safety, status, or a persistent agreement.
Minor = not world-changing but materially establishes character, ordinary life, emotional continuity, inventory, cultural preference, running commitments, or future hooks.
Do not discard minor events merely because they are comedic.

HARD RULES
- Assistant draft is not canon until the user posts it and the game responds.
- A replayed scene is not automatically a second occurrence.
- A status panel cannot silently overwrite stronger established continuity.
- Preserve uncertainty. Do not create causal explanations from outcome alone.
- Preserve attribution boundaries: same mechanism does not prove same author.
- Preserve collaboration credit.
- Player/OOC corrections outrank generated output.
- Timothy remains 1st Year until actual advancement is played.

QUALITY CHECK
Before finishing, ask internally: What changed? What stayed the same? What is only suspected? What will matter three sessions later? What ordinary beat would be painful to lose?

END SKILL
