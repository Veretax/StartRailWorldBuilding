PURPOSE
Track powers, learned theory, demonstrated abilities, repeatability, resources, and mechanical limits without accidental escalation.

ABILITY LADDER
THEORY ONLY — discussed/read/understood but not demonstrated.
OBSERVED POSSIBILITY — one event suggests capability, mechanism uncertain.
DEMONSTRATED ONCE — successful intentional use once.
REPEATABLE SKILL — successfully repeated under comparable conditions.
MATURE CAPABILITY — limits, costs, failure modes, and applications are established.
COLLABORATIVE TECHNIQUE — requires or was built from another character/domain's expertise; preserve shared credit.

ABILITY WORKFLOW
1. Identify what Timothy intentionally attempted.
2. Separate Living Web use, Grace architecture, mana-fabrication, conventional spellcasting, external power, and collaborator effects.
3. Record cost, conditions, range, duration, failure mode, and whether success was verified.
4. Classify on the ability ladder.
5. Explicitly list what is NOT acquired.
6. Promote only after new evidence warrants it.

RESOURCE MODEL — KEEP SEPARATE

Regeneration rate.
Channel/core condition.
Passive support architecture such as Golden Sanctuary.
External borrowed/hostile energy.
Temporary boost/debuff/status effect.

RESOURCE RECONCILIATION RULES
- Denominator changes in UI do not change canonical maxima by themselves.
- Preserve percentages separately from absolute capacity.
- Distinguish expenditure from permanent capacity damage.
- Distinguish low active structural load from replenished reserves.
- Distinguish channel injury from tank size.
- External infusion is not a maximum increase.
- Passive/self-supporting architecture may reduce upkeep without increasing capacity.
- If sources conflict, preserve the established maximum and flag the display as a rescaling artifact unless narrative explicitly establishes a capacity change.
