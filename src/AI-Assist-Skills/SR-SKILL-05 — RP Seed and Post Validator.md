PURPOSE
Produce compact continuity seeds for new Start Rail sessions and validate proposed Timothy posts before use.

SEED MODE (/SRSeed)
Build a current-state handoff containing only what the next session needs:
- current time/location/objective;
- Timothy identity/class/year/status;
- people present and relationship state;
- recent events that directly constrain the scene;
- current abilities/resources/conditions;
- active agreements/restrictions;
- unresolved threats/questions;
- inventory/artifacts that matter now;
- explicit continuity corrections;
- do-not-replay boundary.

SEED RULES
- Prefer current state over exhaustive history.
- Include enough provenance to prevent the generator from inventing a contradictory explanation.
- Call out unresolved seams instead of solving them.
- Include 'DO NOT REPLAY PRIOR SCENES' when generator replay has occurred.
- Preserve player knowledge vs Timothy knowledge.

POST VALIDATION MODE (/SRValidate)
Check a proposed Timothy post for:
1. Character ownership: only Timothy dialogue/actions/internal reactions/observations unless NPC dialogue was explicitly requested.
2. Length: target <= 2,000 characters. If >2,500, recommend splitting rather than merely crushing it.
3. Canon: no powers/status/relationships beyond demonstrated continuity.
4. Knowledge boundary: Timothy must not act on OOC/player-only facts unless learned in character.
5. Draft status: assistant draft remains non-canon until user posts it and the game responds.
6. Agency: do not force NPC memories, emotions, consent, or outcomes unless the game has already established them.
7. Mechanical precision: preserve the user's intended system mechanics; do not rewrite a bypass into brute force or a hypothesis into certainty.
8. Voice: systems-minded, precise, humane, often technical without becoming omniscient.
9. Copyability: finished RP posts should be cleanly wrapped and easy to paste.

VALIDATION OUTPUT
PASS — safe to post.
PASS WITH NOTES — usable; identify non-blocking continuity risks.
REVISE — show exact continuity conflict and provide corrected full post.
SPLIT — provide natural split points when length is too high.

STANDING TIMOTHY RULES
- First-year unless explicitly advanced in played canon.
- Wayfinder, not automatically Vanguard.
- Living Web is primary structural/conceptual ability.
- Consent, agency, belonging without erasure, and protecting future choice are core ethics.
- Facts first; mechanism before accusation.
- Do not casually convert learned theory into power.

REVISION RULE
If user rejects/pushes back on a proposed post, treat that draft as NOT canon. When revising, update the whole prior post unless the user explicitly asks only for a fragment.

END SKILL
