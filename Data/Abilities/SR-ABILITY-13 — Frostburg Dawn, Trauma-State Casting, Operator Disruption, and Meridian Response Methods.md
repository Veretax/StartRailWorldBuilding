RIVER AEGIS — OPERATOR DISRUPTION CONFIRMED
Status: confirmed failure mode.

Implication: defense architecture requires operator-state considerations, not only resource thresholds.
Do not classify this as depletion.

FROSTBURG DAWN
Status: demonstrated emergency technique; one adverse-condition use does not establish mastery.
Mechanism:
- wet cobblestone provides water film;
- Timothy uses environmental mana symbiosis to pull/redirect heat and freeze surface moisture;
- geometry radiates away from nearby allies and toward attackers’ approach;
- minimal/limited CE may define exclusion geometry;
- traction denial is the effect, not direct bodily targeting.
  Safety behavior:
- stops active thermal extraction when Elise reaches him;
- remaining ice is left to ordinary physics.
  Tactical significance:
- works despite unreliable visual targeting because Timothy can act on known ground geometry;
- demonstrates that environmental symbiosis can remain available under cognitive impairment when the structure is simple and deeply remembered.

TRAUMA-STATE CASTING
Observed:
- complex focus-dependent constructs (Aegis) unavailable immediately after head trauma;
- simpler environmental action anchored to autobiographical memory remains possible.
  Interpretation: memory/embodied association can supply a stable conceptual pathway when analytical targeting is degraded.
  Important: this is an observation from one event, not a general rule that trauma improves casting.

ANCHORING UNDER CONCUSSION

  A full energy pool cannot clear a student for field use.
