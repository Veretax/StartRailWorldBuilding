# SR-ABIL-02 — Timothy Maddox Ability Taxonomy — Mana and Magic

## Purpose
Separate Timothy's mana/magic capabilities from Living Web operations. This registry is intentionally conservative: conventional spellcasting must not be inferred from conceptual engineering or accidental mana phenomena.

## Current Baseline
Timothy now possesses measurable mana and a structural filter/hull used to contain and route it. The reviewed material establishes dangerous overpressure behavior, one context-specific True Dragon interaction, and one accidental physical manifestation. It does **not** establish a broad learned witch spell repertoire.

## A. Structural Mana Filter / Conceptual Hull
**Status: ESTABLISHED SUPPORT ARCHITECTURE / AIRA-MENTORED.**

Function:
- contains and buffers internal mana pressure;
- provides structural headroom between Timothy and ambient/high-density magical sources;
- can be monitored for pressure gradient, membrane strain, accumulation, and coupling;
- can be manually bled/depressurized by Aira.

Observed thresholds in current source:
- intended conservative upper operating range discussed around 84–85%;
- 94.2% was treated as dangerous overload, not successful normal operation;
- Eve measured ~89% membrane strain at that state;
- Aira reduced the reservoir to ~65%.

Limits:
- not proof of limitless mana storage;
- elasticity delays failure; it does not eliminate failure;
- high-density magical proximity can reduce remaining margin rapidly;
- filter condition and mana percentage are not interchangeable with combat readiness.

Not established:

## F. Mana Pressure as Unintentional Casting Risk
**Status: ESTABLISHED SAFETY LESSON.**

At severe overpressure, simply refraining from deliberate casting may not be enough. Thought/imagination can provide form to escaping energy. Therefore:
- reduce pressure rather than relying only on behavioral restraint;
- avoid high-complexity visualization when overfilled;
- keep independent telemetry active;
- use qualified magical supervision when structural margin is low.

## G. Conventional Spellcasting Status
**Status: LIMITED / NOT SUFFICIENTLY ESTABLISHED IN REVIEWED RECORDS.**

Timothy is learning within a witch lineage and can reason about mana, resonance, anchors, geometric structure, runes, and magical theory. However, current evidence should not be rewritten into a generic catalog of conventional learned spells.

Until separately demonstrated, do not assume mastery of:
- elemental spell families;
- standard rune casting;
- illusion casting;
- witch-circle rituals;
- healing spells;
- routine familiar creation;
- unrestricted mana shaping.

## Cross-Domain Note

## Safety Spine
**Mana available ≠ mana safe to hold. Mana held ≠ permission to cast. Accidental manifestation under overload ≠ mastery.**
