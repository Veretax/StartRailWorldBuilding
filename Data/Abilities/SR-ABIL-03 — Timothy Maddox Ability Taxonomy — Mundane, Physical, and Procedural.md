# SR-ABIL-03 — Timothy Maddox Ability Taxonomy — Mundane, Physical, and Procedural

## Purpose
Registry of Timothy's non-supernatural capabilities: learned physical skills, engineering/research competencies, security methods, social/observational techniques, and ordinary practices. These should not be mislabeled as Living Web or mana powers simply because Timothy applies the same reasoning style across domains.

## A. Physical / Movement Skills
1. **Physical Flow / Load Routing — TRAINED / DEMONSTRATED.** Heather teaches Timothy to step offline, redirect force, and keep knees/hips/shoulders/core available rather than becoming a rigid impact boundary. Working phrase: **“Let the force go somewhere else.”**
2. **Remove the Span / Evasive Load Avoidance — TRAINED.** When geometry permits, avoid accepting the load at all rather than proving durability.
3. **Cycle the Stress — TRAINED.** Under continuous environmental load, vary gait, angle, and load path so fatigue does not repeatedly concentrate through the same structure.
4. **Basic Field Mobility / Bracing — DEMONSTRATED.** Timothy can jump, brace, climb, move through difficult operational spaces, and work while tethered; supernatural mobility aids remain separately cataloged.
5. **Operational Endurance Awareness — DEVELOPING.** Increasing ability to recognize post-adrenaline crash, caloric/hydration need, and when Medical review is appropriate. This is not yet consistently self-enforcing.

## B. Systems / Engineering Reasoning
1. **Systems Architecture / Dependency Mapping — ESTABLISHED CORE COMPETENCY.** Timothy naturally maps source, path, destination, hidden dependencies, load-bearing seams, rollback, lifecycle and failure domains.
2. **Zero Trust Security Design — ESTABLISHED.** Deny by default, authenticate separately from authorization, least privilege, independent validation, fail-closed behavior, sandboxing, canaries, provenance and bounded access.
3. **Failure-Mode Analysis — ESTABLISHED.** Timothy repeatedly asks how a design fails before trusting nominal operation; includes diagnostic fraction tests, support-dependency stepdowns, failure dumps, and sacrificial components.
4. **Lifecycle / Retirement Thinking — ESTABLISHED.** A tool he built is not automatically his permanent burden. He can document provenance, define last-known-good state, then turn maintenance/retirement over to the legitimate owner.
5. **Forensic Provenance Discipline — ESTABLISHED.** Preserve timestamps, distinguish last known-good state from later changes, freeze scenes, avoid contaminating evidence, and separate observation from interpretation.
6. **Independent-Instrument Method — ESTABLISHED.** Deliberately keep Timothy's conceptual observations and Makina/physical instrumentation independent, then compare results rather than allowing one instrument to define the other.
7. **Magnitude / Unit Verification Habit — ESTABLISHED SAFETY PROTOCOL with Makina.** Suspicious magnitudes can be interrupted and rechecked before execution.

## C. Authentication / Counterintelligence Procedures
1. **Authentication by Contradiction — ESTABLISHED PROCEDURE.** Give a privately generated false premise that a genuine person should reject rather than ask archive trivia a copy may know.
2. **Relational Contradiction Test — ESTABLISHED in Astrit-B case.** Use recent relational context and observe the reflex to reject/correct instead of measuring recall alone.
3. **Identity ≠ Authorization — ESTABLISHED DOCTRINE.** Verify who someone is and separately verify whether the claimed task/order is legitimate.
4. **Subject–Context–Boundary Validation — ESTABLISHED FRAMEWORK.** A subject can be forged inside a corroborating forged context; validate all three.
5. **Do Not Test the Person; Test the Information Path — ESTABLISHED SECURITY DOCTRINE.** Canary/proxy operations target channel compromise rather than turning friends into suspects without evidence.
6. **Need-to-Know Compartmentation — ESTABLISHED.** Share the minimum truthful package required for a legitimate role without inventing false cover stories.
7. **Read-Only / Observation-First Incident Handling — ESTABLISHED.** Freeze, image, snapshot, sandbox, preserve and only then manipulate.

## D. Recon / Observation / Social Analysis
1. **Pattern Observation — ESTABLISHED.** Timothy notices behavior, spatial relationships, audience response, environmental change and what accepted frames omit.
2. **Audience / Substrate Political Analysis — DEMONSTRATED with Miho.** During Luminaris observation he distinguishes loud factional positions from the values revealed by merchants, pedestrians, customers and other bystanders. Working principle: **the argument shows what the factions care about; the audience shows what the place cares about.**
3. **Neutral Labeling Under Uncertainty — ESTABLISHED.** Examples include Astrit-A / Astrit-B and resisting 'mimic' or 'counterfeit' labels until evidence supports them.
4. **Question Reframing — ESTABLISHED.** Timothy often checks whether the stated problem must be accepted before optimizing within it.
5. **Contradiction and Assumption Hunting — ESTABLISHED.** Finds hidden premises in tactical, social, technical and academic frames.
6. **Cross-Domain Analogy — ESTABLISHED.** Rivers, networks, structural loads, engineering, music and social systems are used to build testable understanding. Analogy remains model, not evidence.

## E. Leadership / Coordination
1. **Distributed Team Coordination — ESTABLISHED.** Timothy can maintain a broad picture while leaving local specialists in their lanes instead of physically becoming every team.
2. **Command Deference with Independent Judgment — ESTABLISHED.** He will challenge assumptions or unsafe orders but returns decisions to the authority that legitimately owns them.
3. **Role-Based Delegation — ESTABLISHED.** Uses function and expertise rather than title prestige alone; recognizes when someone else should own maintenance, command, containment or theory.
4. **Smallest Effective Intervention — ESTABLISHED OPERATING HABIT.** Prefer observation, restriction, rerouting and bounded repair before destructive escalation.
5. **Proactive Boundary Setting — DEVELOPING / DEMONSTRATED.** Cancels or defers nonessential high-intensity training, creates sequestration/report blocks, and distinguishes liking a person from accepting every demand on his time.
6. **Incident Closure / Handoff — DEVELOPING.** Increasingly archives channels, identifies residual owners, and asks whether active operations are actually complete.

## F. Research / Academic / Writing Skills
1. **Technical / Conceptual Writing — ESTABLISHED.** Produces reports, frameworks, doctrine, structured analysis and operational records.
2. **Watchman Reflective Writing — ESTABLISHED.** Uses essays to metabolize emotional/institutional experiences without turning them into targeted accusations. 'Load-Bearing' is a key example.
3. **Research Synthesis — ESTABLISHED.** Compares historical sources, theory, telemetry and witness reports while preserving provenance and uncertainty.
4. **Collaborative Theory Building — ESTABLISHED.** Can work with Sophie, Makina, Aira, Ecula and others while preserving authorship distinctions and accepting correction.
5. **Learning by Model → Test → Revision — ESTABLISHED.** Timothy prefers patterns, variables, relationships, equations, examples, counterexamples and failure tests over memorized answer-feeding.
6. **Report / Evidence Organization — ESTABLISHED.** Maintains event timelines, decision trees, disclosure classes, evidence-vs-inference distinctions and lifecycle notes.

## G. Relational / Interpersonal Practices
These are skills/habits, not supernatural effects.

1. **Consent-First Contact — ESTABLISHED pattern.** Ask before touching, scanning, disclosing, recruiting or intensifying closeness when circumstances allow.
2. **Invitation Rather Than Assumption — ESTABLISHED.** Especially visible with Astrit, Skye, Luna, Eve/Makina and specialists whose power/expertise Timothy refuses to treat as automatically available.
3. **Seeing Person Beneath Function — ESTABLISHED value/practice.** Timothy explicitly resists reducing Mecha, dragons, witches, officers, students or himself to utility.
4. **Contribution Without Appropriation — ESTABLISHED.** He can improve another person's system or idea without claiming it as his.
5. **Repair Through Direct Conversation — DEVELOPING.** When trust is injured, Timothy increasingly asks what happened and why before demanding apology or making irreversible decisions.
6. **Ordinary Connection / Hospitality — ESTABLISHED but narratively fragile.** Tea, food, recipes, patisserie visits, games, study and quiet companionship create relationships that are not crisis-only.

## H. Ordinary Practical Skills / Habits
- tea ordering/brewing awareness, especially Earl Grey/vanilla scenes;
- cooking/baking from family recipes, including blondies/brownies and shared food rituals;
- note taking and personal documentation;
- Scripture reading/prayer as a chosen foundational discipline;
- basic computer/inbox use and administrative scheduling;
- humor, science-fiction/pop-culture references, and dry theatrical play as social tools.

These ordinary competencies matter because Timothy's identity must not be reduced to crisis architecture.

## Guardrail
Do not promote a doctrine, personality trait, or one-time lucky result into a power. Conversely, do not erase mundane competence simply because a magical solution was available in the same scene.
