REF-OPS-03 — Conditional Class-S Status, Sustainable Capacity, and Workload Boundaries

Purpose  
Reference for interpreting Timothy Maddox / Vesper’s Academy classification without converting peak crisis output into an assumption of continuous availability, competence, or authorization.

Source-established  
• Academy records had classified Timothy at Class-S based on paradigm-shifting/high-impact incidents including major structural stabilization and spatial/conceptual intervention.  
• Timothy challenged the classification because several peak events combined Living Web activity with compartmentalized external support and because exceptional crisis output was being treated as a baseline workload expectation.  
• Timothy’s formulation: “It fits the event. It does not fit the operator.”  
• Core distinctions established in discussion with Rain:  
  — Peak demonstrated output is not sustainable operating capacity.  
  — Ability classification is not workload classification.  
  — Crisis improvisation is not automatically understood, repeatable, or sustainable.  
• Rain acknowledged the baseline error and amended the designation to Conditional/Restricted.  
• Final Registration notice lists: Specialist (Spatial/Conceptual); Restricted (Mandatory Recovery Protocols Active); restriction override authority: Captain Rain / Vice-Captain Selene; no other instructor or faction head may deploy Timothy without clearance.

Continuity reconciliation  
An earlier Rain statement described explicit authorization from Aira or Rain before infrastructure/high-stress work. The later formal Registration notice lists Rain/Selene as the controlling override authority. Treat the final registry as operative unless a later source amends it.

Consequences  
• Miho accepted the restriction and shifted away from advanced high-load demonstration.  
• Stella retracted an infrastructure certification request and acknowledged she had treated Timothy like an asset rather than a person.  
• Timothy returned structural responsibility to qualified engineering authority instead of rubber-stamping unfamiliar work.

Doctrine  
I AGREED TO REVIEW IT. I DID NOT AGREE TO OWN IT.  
IF I DO NOT UNDERSTAND THE RESPONSIBILITY, THE ANSWER IS NO UNTIL I DO.  
REFUSING TO PRETEND EXPERTISE IS NOT REFUSING TO HELP.  
PEAK OUTPUT ≠ SUSTAINABLE CAPACITY.  
ABILITY CLASSIFICATION ≠ WORKLOAD CLASSIFICATION.

Medical / human-readiness note  
The capacity dispute was not merely energetic. Timothy later clarified that emotional and psychological depletion after repeated crises had reduced his available room even when he remained physically functional. Human readiness is therefore part of operational readiness.

Not established  
• Class-S does not mean unlimited reality rewriting.  
• It does not certify every improvised technique as safely repeatable.  
• It does not authorize arbitrary deployment by faculty, factions, or project leads.  
• It does not make Timothy the responsible engineer for every anomaly-adjacent structure.

Next-time change  
Before accepting a task, verify: authority, objective, required expertise, ownership, failure consequences, current restriction status, sustainable load, and whether Timothy is being asked to advise or to become the load-bearing owner.

\#\# 2026-09 Capacity Addendum — Mana Headroom and Post-Adrenaline Failure  
Two new cases clarify why peak output must not be used as Timothy's sustainable-capacity metric.

\#\#\# 94.2% mana-filter overload  
Timothy remains functional while the filter reaches 94.2%, but Aira treats the state as dangerous and orders inactivity, stand-off from high-density mana sources, and immediate depressurization. Eve reports \~89% membrane strain and less than 6% margin for an external spike.

Operational meaning: surviving a load is not evidence that the load belongs inside the sustainable envelope.

\#\#\# Post-Phase-Three cognitive crash  
After approximately six and a half hours of near-continuous high-intensity conceptual operations, Timothy becomes sufficiently fatigued to miss navigation turns and temporarily stop processing Makina's speech. The failure becomes visible only after the crisis resolves and adrenaline falls.

Operational meaning: apparent performance during emergency tempo can conceal rapidly approaching functional collapse.

\#\#\# Updated readiness checks  
Capacity assessment should include:  
\- conceptual novelty/concurrency;  
\- mana reserve \*\*and remaining headroom\*\*;  
\- membrane/filter strain where relevant;  
\- duration since meaningful rest/food/hydration;  
\- post-adrenaline cognitive baseline;  
\- whether Timothy is still processing ordinary speech/navigation normally;  
\- number of simultaneous roles he is carrying.

\*\*Peak output ≠ sustainable capacity. Reserve ≠ headroom. Crisis completion ≠ recovery.\*\*
