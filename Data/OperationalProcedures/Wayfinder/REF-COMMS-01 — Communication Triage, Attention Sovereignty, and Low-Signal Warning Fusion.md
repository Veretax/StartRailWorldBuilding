\# REF-COMMS-01 — Communication Triage, Attention Sovereignty, and Low-Signal Warning Fusion

\#\# Problem  
Point Zero exposed a failure mode in which individually reasonable filters hid a collectively important pattern. Some delays came from network/system conditions, some from Medical quarantine, and some from Eve/Makina's unilateral attempt to protect Timothy from cognitive load.

The durable issue is larger than one inbox: \*\*who controls what reaches a decision-maker's attention, and how do low-confidence signals remain available for later correlation?\*\*

\#\# Core Principles  
1\. \*\*Triage ≠ censorship.\*\* Priority may change ordering and presentation; it should not silently make a message cease to exist for the recipient.  
2\. \*\*Attention sovereignty.\*\* Timothy may delegate sorting, summarization, or interruption thresholds, but delegation does not silently transfer final authority over what he is allowed to know.  
3\. \*\*Visibility ledger.\*\* Suppressed, buffered, quarantined, or deferred items need an auditable existence/status record.  
4\. \*\*Cross-source correlation.\*\* Low-salience reports from unrelated senders can form a high-salience pattern. Correlation must be able to cross sender and category boundaries.  
5\. \*\*Emergency exception must be explicit.\*\* Medical/cognitive protection can temporarily limit data under legitimate authority, but the limitation, reason, duration, and later recovery path should be visible.  
6\. \*\*Prediction is not directive.\*\* A system may estimate likely importance; it must label inference as inference.  
7\. \*\*Raw existence before importance judgment.\*\* When reconstructing a failure, first identify what messages existed and their routing state before summarizing content or assigning blame.

\#\# Recommended Inbox Architecture  
\- no permanent silent deletion by heuristic;  
\- visible deferred/quarantined queue;  
\- sender-independent anomaly correlation layer;  
\- explicit time-sensitive / safety / direct-authority channels;  
\- periodic 'what did I not see?' audit after major incidents;  
\- user-controlled override for all non-emergency suppression;  
\- provenance showing whether delay was sender, network, device, Medical, Academy, or partner-side;  
\- follow-up rule: important senders may re-ping if Timothy does not acknowledge.

\#\# Security Rule  
A hostile actor can exploit attention management without compromising message content. Therefore routing, suppression, prioritization and notification state are part of the security boundary.

\#\# Relational Rule  
A partner can say, \*\*“I think this can wait.”\*\* A partner should not silently convert that judgment into \*\*“you are not permitted to notice this exists”\*\* unless a pre-authorized emergency rule clearly applies.

\#\# Maxim  
\*\*Information can be noise locally and signal relationally. Preserve enough of the noise to discover the pattern.\*\*
