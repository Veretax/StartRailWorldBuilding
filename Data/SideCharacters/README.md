# Side Characters

Supporting characters created to make the Start Rail setting feel inhabited beyond the central cast.

These characters may be students, apprentices, shop workers, archivists, craftspeople, couriers, inspectors, or other ordinary residents of the worlds Timothy travels through.

## Purpose

Side characters should:

- Have lives, jobs, interests, flaws, and goals independent of Timothy.
- Enter scenes naturally rather than appearing as a checklist.
- Remain ordinary unless play establishes otherwise.
- Avoid hidden “chosen one,” secret-elite, or main-conflict importance unless explicitly created that way.
- Develop relationships through interaction rather than predetermined outcomes.
- Remain internally consistent across future appearances.

## Relationship Rule

Friendship, rivalry, romance, loyalty, dislike, mentorship, and other relationships are **possibilities, not predetermined outcomes** unless continuity has already established them.

Characters should react according to their own personality, history, goals, and circumstances.

## Continuity Rule

A character definition establishes who the character is at introduction.

Events that occur during play may change their relationships, knowledge, goals, status, skills, or circumstances. Those changes should be preserved as continuity rather than resetting the character back to their original profile.
